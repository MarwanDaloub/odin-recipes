Eggs Recipe

Description

Jelly eggs that taste like you never have tasted better eggs before!

    Ingredients
  • Eggs straight from a Chicken
  • Olive oil
    Steps
  1. Pour little bit of olive oil in a pan on low heat.
  2. Crack an egg onto the pan
  3. Cover the pan with a lid and let it cook from above with steam
  4. after 3-4 minutes, Voila!, you got yourself a nice jelly Egg!
