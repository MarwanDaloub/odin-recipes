Bakboka's Recipe

Description

Bakboka is a Libyan national dish, it's a pasta dish that consists of alot of flavours, drenched in pasta special made sauce that explodes in your mouth with all kinds of flavours from various vegetables, and also from the meat it is cooked in.

    Ingredients
  • 300g of Pasta (anykind)
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Table spoon of Tomato paste
  • Half an Onion
  • 1 Potato
  • Salt, black pepper
  • Water
  • Chicken meat, or any preffered meat. (optional)
    Steps
  1. Pour 3 table spoons of vegetable oil into a pan turn the medium heat on the pan
  2. Cut your Onion into little pieces and put them into the pan
  3. Leave the Onions until they get the golden brown color and then put two tablespoons of tomato paste
  4. Put a pinch of salt, and stir the tomato paste in the pan and mix it with the onions
  5. Now put your pieces of meat in the pan and keep stiring everything all together for couple of mins.
  6. Fill the pan with water until the meat is fully covered, then put the vegetables of your choice, cut the potato into small cubes and put them in the pan
  7. Leave the pan cooking for half an hour, check on it from time to time, when you think the water is going into low levels pour more water
  8. When the potato and the meat are fully cooked, pour your pasta into the pan and leave it until the pasta is cooked
  9. Voila!, now you have a nice plate of bakboka
