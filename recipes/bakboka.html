Bakboka's Recipe

Description

Bakboka is a Libyan national dish, it's a pasta dish that consists of alot of flavours, drenched in pasta special made sauce that explodes in your mouth with all kinds of flavours from various vegetables, and also from the meat it is cooked in.

    Ingredients
  • 300g of Pasta (anykind)
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Table spoon of Tomato paste
  • 1 Onion head
  • 1 Potato
  • Salt, black pepper
  • Water
  • Chicken meat, or any preffered meat.
